# Communication reminder

- Explain results in ordinary language first. Do not assume the user knows
  machine-learning terms such as "fold", "sparse feature", "overfitting",
  "challenger", or "support gate".
- Stay concise. Replacing jargon with plain language should not make the answer
  longer than necessary.
- When a technical term is genuinely useful, define it immediately and connect
  it to the concrete UFC example, including the relevant fight counts and what
  practical decision follows.
- Prefer "what happened, why it happened, and what it means" over compressed
  research shorthand. A result should make sense without requiring the reader
  to decode model-development vocabulary.
