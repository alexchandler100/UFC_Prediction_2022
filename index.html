Past Fights (Recent)

We started publishing fight forecasts on April 23, 2022. Below we show the history of forecasts made before each event. For reference, available sportsbook prices are also shown. New records use a no-vig consensus in probability space; older records may contain the site's legacy arithmetic odds average. After each event, the table is automatically updated to indicate the winners in gold and keep a running total of the prediction accuracy. Correctly predicted fights are indicated in green and incorrectly predicted fights are shown in red. Historical accuracy is descriptive only: this table spans multiple model versions, and older rows may lack stable fight IDs or complete provenance. It is not a betting-performance guarantee.

The machine learning model implemented on this page will analyze all known fighter stats (scraped from ufcstats.com) and predict the most likely outcome if they were to fight exactly as they were on the specified dates. Details on how these models were constructed can be found here on our Github. This experimental browser model is not the validated weekly model and has no current accuracy claim. All photos are scraped from Google image search acted on by a convolutional neural network (CNN) for artistic style transfer (8,964 photos total). Weekly sportsbook prices are retrieved through The Odds API; the experimental calculator accepts manual inputs from any source.

We do not recommend you make financial decisions based on the outcomes of this program
